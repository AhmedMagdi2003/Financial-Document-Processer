# Demo Narration Script

The final voiceover is in English (US neural voice). The script below is spoken over the screen capture. The voice-rendering step sends only this narration text to Microsoft's online TTS service, not the application data.

## 00:00–00:12 — Introduction

“This is a preview of unified currency reporting in the Financial Document Assistant. The expense entries in this isolated demo workspace are fictional.”

## 00:12–00:29 — Select reporting currency

“Choose US dollars, euros, or Egyptian pounds from the selector at the top of the application. The confirmed-spend total updates into the selected reporting currency. You can switch the reporting view without changing the source records.”

## 00:29–00:45 — Unified expense view

“Each expense keeps its original invoice amount alongside its converted reporting value. Spend from different source currencies is combined in the category and monthly summaries, making one clear comparison across currencies.”

## 00:45–01:05 — Exchange-rate details

“The details panel shows source-currency totals, the currency pairs, and the provider's update timestamp. In this capture, the live rates came from ExchangeRate-API Open Access. The date makes it clear when that rate set was published.”

## 01:05–01:22 — Close

“Currency conversion here is for reporting only. It does not rewrite the source invoice or the amount stored in the expense record. These sample expenses are fictional, and this short preview focuses on currency reporting.”

When recording currency values, read the pair and date shown in the live application. They change over time; do not substitute a remembered or hard-coded rate. This preview does not demonstrate AI extraction, human-review resolution, or external Drive/Sheets delivery.
